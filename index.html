FAQS

Questions, answered.

A few of the most common questions before starting a custom order.

ASK A QUESTION →
Can I order a sample?

Yes. A sample can be produced so you can review fit, stitching, fabric and print quality before bulk production.

What is the minimum order?

MOQ varies by product and design. Contact us with your requirements for the current quantity options.

Do you offer fully custom designs?

Yes. You can provide a tech pack, design file, mockup, reference image or simply an idea and we can work from it.

Where do you ship?

Worldwide, including the USA, UK, Canada, Australia and Europe.
